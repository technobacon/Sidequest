# Competitors and Sponsors — September 2026

Research date: 2026-09-07

## Closest competitors

### 1. Damon Cassidy
- ~322K subscribers.
- Very close audience overlap: Gen Z economics, careers, cost of living, work, financial independence, modern adulthood.
- Major 2026 outliers include `Why Gen Z GAVE UP Having Kids` (~2.0M), `WTF Is Happening To Entry Level Jobs` (~1.4M), and `Gen Z Has Become UNEMPLOYABLE` (~1.27M).
- Confirmed sponsor example: Zocdoc on `Gen Z Has Become UNEMPLOYABLE`.
- Repeated 80,000 Hours career CTA/partnership also appears in several job-market videos.
- Biggest lesson: concrete cultural/economic pain framed around one generation is currently extremely clickable.

### 2. Early Exit
- ~50K subscribers as of the latest indexed channel result.
- Closest production-format reference: strongly packaged life/money/work arguments over simple gameplay.
- `Gen Z is Getting Priced Out of Their Own Country` reached ~678K after being uploaded when the channel had only ~2.3K subscribers.
- The searched breakout description had no obvious sponsor integration; the channel is more important as proof of the low-edit format than as sponsorship proof.

### 3. Better Ideas
- ~3.04M subscribers; SponsorRadar reports ~197K average views.
- Broad self-improvement / life strategy with strong anti-guru and anti-overoptimization angles.
- Recent sponsors detected: Soundcore, Incogni, Ritual, Higgsfield, Nootrify; historical list also includes Hostinger, Skillshare, Headspace, Shortform and Athletic Greens.
- Strong current topics: damaged attention, overthinking, self-improvement backlash, procrastination.

### 4. Nate O'Brien
- ~1.28M subscribers.
- Strong overlap between personal finance, minimalism, financial independence and intentional living.
- Sponsorship history includes finance brands such as Betterment, Fundrise, M1 Finance, SoFi, Trading 212, Webull, Cash App and others.
- Current publishing cadence is much slower, but the audience/positioning remains a useful reference.

### 5. The Financial Diet
- ~1.34M subscribers.
- Money through culture, class, consumption and everyday life rather than pure investing.
- Current/recent sponsor example: Betterment.
- Strong topic overlap with Sidequest: consumerism, middle class, marriage economics, retirement, influencer economy, quality/cheapness.

## Additional direct competitors to track

These were identified directly as relevant Sidequest competitors and should be included in future competitor and trend scans:

- **WuckaSpeaks**
- **niko**
- **MonomoTime**
- **KellsterNow**

Treat these as direct competitive signals alongside Damon Cassidy and Early Exit. For each, track:
- new uploads and first 24–72 hour view velocity
- breakout videos relative to the channel's own baseline
- repeated themes and audience pain points
- title/thumbnail patterns
- topic overlap with money, work, wellbeing, self-improvement, and modern-life advice
- sponsor integrations when visible

Do not assume their strongest topics remain static; re-research their current catalogues during future scans.

## Large adjacent competitors

- Recent sponsors: NOCD and Lee Holden.
- Relevant current titles include `Why Normal Life Feels So Boring` and `Why You Can't Just "Rewire" Your Brain`.

### Matt D'Avella
- ~4.04M subscribers.
- Minimalism, intentional living, productivity and creator life.
- Productivity sponsorship research places Canva and Riverside among brands working with him; Ganbaru Method has also sponsored the channel.
- Current brand direction increasingly overlaps creator wellbeing / sustainable creation.

### struthless
- ~1.23M subscribers.
- Creative life, habits, ADHD, digital wellbeing, self-improvement skepticism.
- Productivity sponsorship research includes Canva and DeleteMe among brands sponsoring the channel.

### Nathaniel Drew / No Backup Plan
- Main channel ~1.78M subscribers; experimental `No Backup Plan` channel ~142K.
- Strong philosophical/life-design overlap.
- SponsorRadar reports No Backup Plan sponsors including Skillshare, Notion, Holafly, Saily, Storyblocks and Incogni, despite only ~21K average views.

### Ali Abdaal

- Broad productivity, work, business, creator and life-design audience.
- Recent sponsor data includes WisprFlow, Shortform, Trading 212, Claude, Skillshare and Flow.
- Huge competitor for the broader `build a better life / better work` audience, but Sidequest can be much less polished and less guru-like.

### Shelby Church
- ~1.84M subscribers.
- Lifestyle, money, creator work, tech, housing and career-adjacent commentary.
- Recent sponsors include Helix Sleep, Emergent, Booking.com and Belkin.

- Especially relevant once Sidequest starts making YouTube/creator advice.

## Current breakout themes

1. **Gen Z / job-market breakdown** — extremely strong. Damon Cassidy repeatedly produces 4–6x subscriber-count outliers around entry-level jobs, AI, layoffs and cost of living.
2. **AI + career insecurity** — layoffs, LinkedIn, entry-level work, whether a safe career still exists.
3. **Priced-out adulthood** — housing, kids, marriage, retirement, middle-class life, cost of normal milestones.
4. **Self-improvement backlash** — overthinking, optimization fatigue, attention, the dark side of self-improvement.
5. **Everyday economics / anti-consumption** — why normal goods feel worse, why buying less feels better, status consumption, lifestyle inflation.
6. **Normal life feels wrong / boring** — quarter-life crisis, work for 40 years, meaning, attention, social life.
7. **Creator independence** — starting YouTube, building something online, quitting/reshaping work; strongest once Sidequest has its own credibility.

## Strategic implication for Sidequest

Do not compete as a generic self-improvement channel. The most promising wedge is evidence-backed commentary on the pressure points of modern adult life, delivered in a casual friend-like voice.

The strongest initial overlap is:
- Damon Cassidy's cultural/economic relevance
- Early Exit's production simplicity
- Better Ideas' self-improvement packaging
- Nate O'Brien's calm money/life tone
- The Financial Diet's everyday-economics framing
- WuckaSpeaks, niko, MonomoTime, and KellsterNow as additional direct competitor signals supplied for ongoing monitoring

Sidequest can differentiate with calm Minecraft gameplay + vintage newspaper graphics + tighter research + less guru certainty.
