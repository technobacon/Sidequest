# Sidequest Channel Strategy

## Core niche

Young-adult life strategy / life-design commentary.

The channel is not tied to finance. Personal finance is one content pillar among several. The broader subject is how to build a good life under modern conditions.

## Audience

Likely core viewer:
- roughly 20–35
- internet-native
- educated or career-oriented
- somewhat skeptical of the default career/life path
- interested in money but not necessarily investing content
- wants more autonomy, time and a better day-to-day life
- dislikes hustle-guru framing
- responds to calm, analytical, practical commentary

## Content pillars

1. **Money and independence**
   - saving, spending, financial freedom, lifestyle inflation, enoughness

2. **Work and careers**
   - career ambition, job security, AI, burnout, management, remote work, opting out

3. **Wellbeing**
   - sleep, exercise, stress, attention, habits, social life, over-optimization

4. **Life design**
   - adulthood, consumption, time, status, relationships with work, choosing a path

5. **Creating online / YouTube**
   - only as credibility grows; framed as life/career strategy rather than technical creator tutorials

## Unifying filter

A topic fits if the same viewer who clicked a money video could plausibly care about it because it helps them make a better life decision.

Do not become a miscellaneous tutorial channel. Avoid jumping from narrow tax mechanics to gym programming to DaVinci Resolve tips unless the topic can be reframed around the broader Sidequest worldview.

## Packaging

Strong videos should usually contain:
- a recognizable life tension
- a concrete contradiction
- personal relevance
- a clear claim or question
- enough evidence to justify the claim

Prefer titles that feel like arguments worth hearing rather than textbook topics.

Examples:
- Why I Don’t Want a Successful Career
- Why Saving Money Feels Pointless Now
- Is This Really What the Next 40 Years Look Like?
- You Probably Don’t Need a Side Hustle
- When Did a Normal Life Become a Luxury?
- We Somehow Made Sleeping Stressful
- The First $100,000 Changes Your Life
- Your Phone Is Eating Your Free Time
- Maybe Your Job Should Just Be a Job
- Why Buying Less Feels So Good

## Monetization thesis

The audience is potentially attractive to sponsors because it overlaps with finance, productivity, careers, wellness, technology and creator tools.

A blended long-form RPM could plausibly sit above broad gaming while below pure finance, depending heavily on geography and topic mix. Sponsorships may become more important than AdSense if the channel develops a reliable 20–35 professional audience.
