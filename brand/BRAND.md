# Sidequest Brand

## Name

**Sidequest**

The name should feel broad enough for money, work, wellbeing, YouTube, habits, creativity and general life advice. Gaming is part of the visual delivery, not the subject of the brand.

## Core line

**Figuring out life, I guess.**

This should stay intentionally human and understated rather than polished or guru-like.

## Visual direction

Keep the vintage newspaper / editorial identity developed for Franklin, but remove the Benjamin Franklin mascot and finance-specific imagery.

### Typography
- Primary: Crimson Pro
- Supporting font: clean neutral sans serif when needed

### Palette
- Warm off-white / aged paper
- Black / charcoal
- One bright red accent

### Channel icon
A simplified hand-sketched compass in vintage newspaper / engraving style:
- black and white overall
- north indicator in bright red
- visible vertical and horizontal guide lines
- slightly imperfect pen-and-ink construction
- simpler and warmer than a formal nautical compass
- should feel welcoming, familiar and like finding your way home

### Banner
A restrained newspaper masthead:
- pale newspaper collage / column texture in the background
- large centered Crimson Pro-style serif text
- exact line: `Figuring out life, I guess.`
- no compass on the banner
- minimal, editorial, low-contrast background

## Tone
Sidequest should sound like a thoughtful friend who has spent time looking into something, not a coach, guru or motivational speaker.

Avoid:
- polished corporate language
- exaggerated certainty
- generic self-help slogans
- pretending everything is figured out
- forced inspiration

Prefer:
- specific observations
- practical implications
- personal uncertainty where genuine
- understated humor
- clear opinions with evidence
